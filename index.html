our story Seven years · four chapters
October 2019

A borrowed table

A shared table at a crowded café in Dhanmondi. Zayan asked if the seat was taken; Mahira said yes — and then, thankfully, changed her mind.

June 2021

The coast road

Ten days, one borrowed car, and the whole marine drive from Cox's Bazar to Teknaf. Somewhere past Inani it stopped being a trip and started being a life.

December 2023

The question

A winter evening at a tea-garden resort in Sreemangal, fog rolling down the slopes, candles lit. Zayan asked. Mahira said yes before he could finish the sentence.

September 2026

The answer continues

Seven years, three apartments, one very opinionated cat. Now: all of you, under the string lights, as the next chapter begins.
